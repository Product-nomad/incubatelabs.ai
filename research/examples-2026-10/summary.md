# Small-business automation examples: summary (October 2026)

Research only. No site files were changed.

**Sources:** 20 of the 121 videos on @AutomateAIConsulting, the top 40 n8n templates in each of three category groups (5,685 templates scanned), Zapier's popular templates for 7 apps, and DataForSEO UK volumes. DataForSEO spend: **$0.20** of the $2 cap. All 118 rows are in `examples.csv`.

## What stands out

- **Demand follows "software", not "automation".** Every "[industry] automation" term has no measurable UK volume (except recruitment automation: 390). Buyers search "bookkeeping software" (720), "letting agent software" (480) and "job management software" (480). So pages should be about the job the software does, not about "automation".
- **The useful "automate" terms are small but easy to rank for:** invoice automation (110, KD 0), automate reports (90, KD 0), automate quotes (50, KD 0). Adjacent terms carry more volume: ai receptionist (1,300), accounts payable automation (390), ai email assistant (390), quoting software (320).
- **Every credible example has a person checking the work.** The model drafts, and a person approves anything that goes to a customer, prices a job or moves money. That's the pitch.
- **The YouTube channel is US-based and sells on revenue, not hours.** Its claims are the creator's own worked examples in dollars, not audited results. Only five rows have measured time savings (all from the creator's former employer): 3 hours a month cut to 15 minutes, 2 hours a week, 10–15 hours a week, about 173 hours a year, and about 100 hours a year.
- **There's a gap:** timesheets, job sheets and purchase orders had almost no examples in any source, but "timesheet app" gets 390 UK searches a month.
- **Neither platform publishes usage counts.** n8n shows views; Zapier shows a "popular" order with no numbers.
