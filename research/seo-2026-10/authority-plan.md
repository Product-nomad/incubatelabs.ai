# Authority and local plan: incubatelabs.ai

This plan is for a new one-person AI and automation service selling to owner-run UK businesses with 5–50 staff. Everything below is free, or cheap, and realistic for one person to do alongside client work.

Where a programme's entry rules weren't verified in this research, the entry says **check current criteria**. Don't treat any listing here as guaranteed.

## The evidence that shapes this plan

- **The niche is winnable on authority.** Of the 13 terms rated realistic for page one in `serp-analysis.csv`, most have several ranking domains with fewer than 100 estimated UK visits a month. The leaders aren't strong sites; they're sites that exist and are specific.
- **Directories and listicles appear in the money SERPs:**
  - Clutch ranks #7 for *ai consulting services*.
  - Sortlist ranks for *ai consultant near me*.
  - Upwork ranks twice for *ai consultant*.
  - Small "best AI consultancies / agencies UK" listicles take 3–4 of the top 10 for *ai consultancy uk*, *ai automation agency uk* and *ai consultant near me*.
  - Being listed on those pages is worth more than ranking against them.
- **The competitors have almost no authority:**
  - newlens.uk: 1 ranking keyword.
  - openkit.co.uk: 23 keywords.
  - cloudswitched.com ranks for 363 keywords, mostly through programmatic location pages and Microsoft 365 guides.
  - createwith.com is a tool and event directory, not a competitor. It's a citation source (see section 2).

## 4. Links from your GitHub projects

The site's Work page links out to `attestloop`, `idea-incubator` and `mysankeycom`. Make the links run both ways:

1. **GitHub profile:** the website field already lists incubatelabs.ai.
   - **Change the bio**, which currently reads *"Open to PM and Delivery roles"*. A buyer who checks your GitHub will read that as "this consultancy is a stopgap". Suggested: *"Applied AI builder. I run Incubate Labs: practical AI and automation for UK small businesses."*
2. **Every public repo README:** add a footer line: *Built by [Simon Newton](https://incubatelabs.ai/about/) at [Incubate Labs](https://incubatelabs.ai).*
   - Set each repo's **Website** field to its own site if it has one (attestloop already points to attestloop.ai), or to `https://incubatelabs.ai/work/`.
   - GitHub links are nofollow, but they build entity association and bring referral clicks from developers who recommend suppliers.
3. **Your own product sites** (attestloop.ai, sessionguard.dev): add a small "Built by Incubate Labs" footer link using brand anchor text. These are real, followed links from relevant domains; keep the anchor text as the brand, not a keyword.
4. **Write-ups:** post a short technical write-up of each project on dev.to or Hashnode, with the canonical URL set to a matching page on incubatelabs.ai (e.g. `/work/attestloop/`) so you get the links without duplicate content. A *Show HN* for attestloop is a reasonable one-off.
5. **Package registries:** if any project is on npm or PyPI, set `homepage` to the project's page on incubatelabs.ai.

## 5. Should you set up a Google Business Profile?

**Yes, as a service-area business with the address hidden.** The evidence:

- **The local pack appears on 5 of the 40 analysed SERPs, and all five are core commercial terms:**
  - ai consultancy uk (170)
  - ai automation agency uk (210)
  - ai automation agency (720)
  - ai business consultant (140)
  - ai consultant near me (110)
  - Google treats these as local-intent searches even when the searcher doesn't type a place. Without a profile you can't appear in that pack at all.
- **Cambridge-specific demand is close to zero:**
  - *ai consultant cambridge* and *ai consultant cambridgeshire* returned no measurable volume.
  - *artificial intelligence cambridge* (320) is academic.
  - *cambridge consultants* (3,600) is a navigational search for a company called Cambridge Consultants.
  - So the profile isn't about ranking for "Cambridge"; it's about appearing in the local pack for the national terms above, for searchers within reach of Huntingdon.
- **Reviews:** a profile is the easiest place to collect client reviews. Reviews also lift click-through on branded searches, and they feed AI answers about "AI consultants near me".

**How to set it up:**
- Choose **service-area business** and hide the address. Google allows this when you don't serve customers at your premises.
- Service areas: Huntingdonshire, Cambridge, Peterborough, Bedford, St Neots, plus "Cambridgeshire" and neighbouring counties. Google allows up to 20.
- **Primary category:** test "Business management consultant" against "Consultant". Look at what the profiles in the local pack for *ai consultancy uk* use and match the most common one.
- Add services that mirror the six use-case pages, using the renamed versions in `page-map.md`.
- Link the profile to the homepage. Post monthly: each new guide is a post.
- Expect video verification.

**On showing a location on the site:**
- Keep exactly what the site does now: *"Based near Huntingdon, Cambridgeshire, working with businesses across the UK."* That matches a service-area profile, reassures buyers that you're a real UK business, and doesn't publish a home address.
- **Don't** add a street address.
- **Don't** create city landing pages ("AI consultant Cambridge", "AI consultant Peterborough"). There's no search demand for them, and thin location pages are exactly what Google demotes. Cloudswitched's programmatic location pages earn traffic in the low single digits each.

## 6. Earned links and AI-answer visibility (months 2–3 onwards)

1. **Get onto the listicles that already rank.** For each "best AI consultancies / agencies UK" page in the Phase 2 top 10, send a short, specific note asking to be considered, with a one-line description and the Readiness Check as proof of work. Targets:
   - aisuperior.com (Cambridge, Plymouth and Manchester lists)
   - nicchin.com
   - learn.bluecanvas.ai
   - softblues.io
   - elevateai.co.uk
   - lotusbrainsstudio.com
   - antekautomation's blog
   - espressio.ai (AI consulting for small businesses list)
2. **The AI policy template as a linkable asset.** Pitch it to the Growth Hub, the FSB branch, the Chamber, AccountingWEB and the trade bodies. Free templates for small businesses earn links because they help the publisher's members.
3. **Be the cited source in AI Overviews.** 37 of the 40 SERPs show an AI Overview, and the sources cited for *ai consultant* include openkit.co.uk, a site with about 14 visits a month. Short, plain definitions with UK prices (the "AI consultant cost" guide) are what gets cited. Put the answer in the first two sentences under each H2.
4. **Two case studies with numbers** ("8 hours a week of invoice keying removed for a 12-person builder") as soon as you have permission. They're the most-linked and most-cited page type for small consultancies, and they fill the empty E-E-A-T gap identified in the technical audit.

## What not to do

- Don't buy links, guest-post packages or "AI directory submission" bundles.
- Don't create a profile on every directory above in week one. Prioritise the Google profile, Bing, Apple, Clutch, GoodFirms, Sortlist and Yell, then add more if time allows.
- Don't put keyword-stuffed anchors in footers on your own product sites. Use brand anchor text only.
