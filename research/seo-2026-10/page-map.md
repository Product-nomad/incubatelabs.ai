# Keyword-to-page map: incubatelabs.ai

Sources: `keywords.csv` (Phase 1), `serp-analysis.csv` (Phase 2) and `competitor-gap.csv` (Phase 3). All data is Google UK, English, pulled 11 October 2026.

**How to read the numbers:**
- **Volume** is average monthly UK searches. **KD** is DataForSEO keyword difficulty on a 0–100 scale; a dash means there was no score.
- Google merges close variants into one demand pool, so "ai consultant", "ai consultants" and "ai consultancy" are one pool of about 2,400 searches, not three. Never add variant volumes together.
- UK volumes in this niche are small. A page targeting 50–200 searches a month is normal and worthwhile when the intent is a buyer looking for help.

Each term is mapped to **one** page. Character counts include spaces and were measured by script: every title is 60 characters or fewer and every meta description 155 or fewer.
