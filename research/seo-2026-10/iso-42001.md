## What it means

- Both SERPs are dominated by certification bodies (LRQA, BSI, Amtivo, TÜV SÜD, NQA), training
  sellers and compliance-software vendors, all selling certification. Only one result (Acato's cost
  guide) is written for SMEs, and none asks whether a small business needs certification at all.
- That's the gap: "Do you need ISO 42001? A plain-English guide for small businesses", answering
  what it is, who actually needs it, what customers ask for instead, what certification involves
  and costs (in hours and effort, no prices), and the lighter route (AI policy, AI register,
  training record, evidence pack). It links naturally to the AI policy template and the EU AI Act
  guide's new certification FAQ.
- Difficulty is low for the volume (KD 20–28), but the ranking pages are on high-authority domains,
  so the realistic target is the long tail and the AI Overview citations rather than position 1
  for "iso 42001".
