# Tenant repair requests, sorted before anyone opens them

**Worked example.** The business and figures below are illustrative, based on a typical firm of this size. We measure yours in the first conversation.

## The business

A 12-person letting agent managing around 300 properties. Repair requests arrive by email all day, from a dripping tap to no heating in January. About 25 a day.

## How it works today

1. Every email is read in the order it arrived, so urgent problems can sit behind routine ones.
2. Someone works out which property it is, how urgent it is, and which contractor to call.
3. They reply to the tenant, log the job and email the contractor.
4. Around 6 minutes each, and more when the email is vague.

## What we'd build

1. **Each repair email is read as it arrives** and matched to the property and tenancy.
2. **It's sorted by urgency** using your own rules. No heating or hot water, leaks, electrical danger and security problems go to the top, and so does anything mentioning children, elderly or vulnerable occupants.
3. **A draft reply to the tenant is written** in your tone, with sensible next steps and any safety advice your policy includes.
4. **A contractor is suggested** from your approved list, by trade and area, with a draft job email.
5. **A person checks both drafts and sends them.** The job is logged against the property.

## Where a person checks

- Every reply and every contractor instruction is sent by a person.
- Urgent cases alert the team straight away rather than waiting in a queue.
- Anything the system can't place, such as an unknown address or unclear problem, goes to a person untouched.

## Time back

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Repair emails a day | 25 | 25 |
| Time each | about 6 minutes | about 2 minutes to check and send |
| Time a day | about 2.5 hours | about 50 minutes |

**About 8 hours a week back**, and the urgent cases are seen first. Our working: 25 emails × 4 minutes saved × 5 days = 500 minutes.

## What it runs on

Your existing inbox, property records and contractor list. AI reads the email and drafts the replies. The urgency rules are yours, written down and visible, so you can see exactly why something was marked urgent.

## What it doesn't do

It doesn't instruct contractors, promise repair dates or reply to tenants by itself. A person stays responsible for every message.
