# Supplier invoices into Xero, without anyone typing them

**Worked example.** The business and figures below are illustrative, based on a typical firm of this size. We measure yours in the first conversation.

## The business

A 15-person building firm running several jobs at once. Materials come from a handful of merchants and hire companies. About 150 supplier invoices arrive each month, mostly as PDF attachments.

## How it works today

1. Invoices land in a shared inbox, mixed in with everything else.
2. Someone opens each PDF, types the supplier, date, reference and VAT into Xero, and works out which job it belongs to.
3. Around 3 minutes an invoice, when nothing is wrong. Mismatches with what was ordered are often spotted late, if at all.

## What we'd build

1. **Invoices are picked out of the inbox automatically**, from known suppliers and new ones.
2. **Each invoice is read**: supplier, invoice number, date, VAT rate, and the job or purchase order reference.
3. **It's checked against your purchase orders or job list.** Matching invoices are coded to the right account and job.
4. **It's waiting in Xero as a draft bill**, with the PDF attached, for a person to approve in batches.
5. **Anything unusual goes to a person**: an unknown supplier, a missing job reference, or a price that doesn't match the order.

## Where a person checks

- Nothing is approved or paid automatically. Bills arrive in Xero as drafts.
- Every exception is sent to a named person with the reason it was flagged.
- Spot checks on a sample each week, so you can see the accuracy for yourself.

## Time back

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Invoices a month | 150 | 150 |
| Keying and coding | about 3 minutes each, 7.5 hours | none for clean invoices |
| Exceptions and review | included above | about 1.5 hours |

**About 6 hours a month back**, and mismatches are caught when the invoice arrives, not at month end. Our working: 150 × 3 minutes = 7.5 hours, against roughly 20 exceptions at 3 minutes plus 30 minutes of batch review.

## What it runs on

Your existing inbox and Xero, connected through Xero's official integration. AI is used only to read the documents. The matching and coding rules are ordinary, predictable automation that you can see and change.

## What it doesn't do

It doesn't approve or pay bills, and it doesn't guess which job an invoice belongs to when the reference is missing. It asks.
