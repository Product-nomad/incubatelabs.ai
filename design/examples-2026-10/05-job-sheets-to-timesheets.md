# Paper job sheets into timesheets and invoice lines, without retyping

**Worked example.** The business and figures below are illustrative, based on a typical firm of this size. We measure yours in the first conversation.

## The business

A 25-person maintenance contractor with 12 engineers on the road. Engineers fill in paper job sheets, and many like it that way. The office retypes them into timesheets for payroll and into invoice lines for customers.

## How it works today

1. About 60 job sheets come back each week, some by photo, some in a pile on Friday.
2. The office retypes hours, materials and job details twice: once for payroll, once for invoicing.
3. Around 4 minutes a sheet, plus chasing the ones that never arrive.

Before buying a new timesheet app and retraining 12 engineers, it's worth asking whether the paper can stay and the retyping go.

## What we'd build

1. **Engineers photograph the sheet** at the end of the job and send it, as they may already do.
2. **The sheet is read**: job reference, date, start and finish times, materials used and notes.
3. **Hours go into the timesheet** for payroll, and **materials and labour become draft invoice lines** against the job.
4. **Missing sheets are chased automatically**: a reminder to the engineer if a booked job has no sheet by the next morning.
5. **The office checks a summary** and approves it.

## Where a person checks

- Anything hard to read is flagged with the photo alongside, not guessed.
- Timesheets are approved by the office before payroll.
- Invoice lines are drafts until someone approves them.

## Time back

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Job sheets a week | 60 | 60 |
| Retyping | about 4 minutes each, 4 hours | none for clear sheets |
| Chasing missing sheets | about 1 hour | automatic |
| Checking | included above | about 1 hour |

**About 4 hours a week back**, and invoices go out sooner because the lines are ready when the job closes. Our working: 4 hours retyping plus 1 hour chasing, against 1 hour of checking.

## What it runs on

Phones, the spreadsheet or job list you already keep, and your accounts software. AI reads the handwriting. Everything after that is ordinary automation.

## What it doesn't do

It doesn't change how engineers work on site or replace payroll. Nothing reaches a payslip or an invoice without a person's approval.
