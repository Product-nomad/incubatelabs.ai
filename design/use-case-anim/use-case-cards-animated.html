The story in each card

Orange is the manual work from the hero: the pile of paper, the flagged question. Green is the automated result. Where a person should check, a small person icon appears with a tick, the same promise the hero makes.

When they play

Once, as each card scrolls into view, staggered so the grid doesn't all move at once. Hover or keyboard focus replays a card. With reduced motion switched on, every card shows its finished state and nothing moves.

Weight

Inline SVG with CSS keyframes and about 30 lines of script. No libraries and no images, and only transform, opacity and stroke offsets are animated.
