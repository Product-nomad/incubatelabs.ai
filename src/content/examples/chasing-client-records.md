# Year-end records chased for you, client by client

**Worked example.** The business and figures below are illustrative, based on a typical firm of this size. We measure yours in the first conversation.

## The business

A 10-person accountancy practice with around 200 clients on annual accounts and self-assessment. Every season, the team spends weeks chasing bank statements, receipts and mileage logs.

## How it works today

1. Each client gets a generic reminder, and most ignore it.
2. Someone checks the folder, works out what's still missing, and writes to the client.
3. Most clients need about three chasers, at around 5 minutes each.
4. Records arrive in the last fortnight and everything piles up at once.

## What we'd build

1. **Each client's folder or portal is checked against a checklist** for their type of work: bank statements for every account, sales and purchase records, and so on.
2. **A personalised "still missing" list is drafted** for each client, naming the exact items and months.
3. **Reminders go out on a schedule** you choose, getting firmer as the deadline nears, with a direct link to upload.
4. **The team approves each batch** before it's sent, and sees a single list of who is complete, who is close and who needs a phone call.

## Where a person checks

- Every batch of reminders is approved before sending.
- Clients who don't respond after the last reminder move to a call list for a person.
- Client data stays in your existing systems. Nothing is copied into tools without proper data protection terms.

## Time back

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Clients | 200 | 200 |
| Chasers | about 3 each at 5 minutes, 50 hours | drafted automatically |
| Approving and follow-up calls | included above | about 10 hours |

**About 40 hours back each season**, and records arrive earlier because each client knows exactly what's missing. Our working: 200 clients × 3 chasers × 5 minutes = 50 hours, against about 10 hours of approval and calls.

## What it runs on

Your practice software or client list, your email and your document storage. AI is used only to read file names and documents to work out what has arrived. The checklist and schedule are yours.

## What it doesn't do

It doesn't do the bookkeeping, give tax advice or contact clients without approval.

A **Quick Win** on one client type, such as sole traders, before the busy season, then extended once the team trusts the lists.
