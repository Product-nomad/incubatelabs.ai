# Overdue invoices chased in the right tone, without the dread

**Worked example.** The business and figures below are illustrative, based on a typical firm of this size. We measure yours in the first conversation.

## The business

A 20-person wholesaler selling to around 150 trade customers on 30-day terms. About 60 invoices go overdue each month. Nobody enjoys chasing them, so it slips.

## How it works today

1. Someone runs the aged debtors report when they remember.
2. For each overdue invoice they check the history, look for disputes, and write an email.
3. Around 8 minutes each. Good customers sometimes get the same blunt email as persistent late payers.

## What we'd build

1. **Each morning, overdue invoices are pulled from Xero.**
2. **Each one is checked for context**: customer history, previous reminders, any notes about disputes or part-payments.
3. **A reminder is drafted in the right tone**: a friendly nudge for a good customer who's a few days late, a firmer note with a copy of the invoice at 30 days, and a flag for a phone call beyond that.
4. **A person reviews the morning's drafts** in one list, edits any that need it and sends them.
5. **Disputed invoices are never chased automatically.** They go straight to a person.

## Where a person checks

- Every reminder is approved before it's sent.
- Disputes, part-payments and key accounts always go to a person.
- The tone rules are yours and you can see them.

## Time back

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Overdue invoices a month | 60 | 60 |
| Time each | about 8 minutes | about 2 minutes to review and send |
| Time a month | about 8 hours | about 2 hours |

**About 6 hours a month back**, and chasing happens every day instead of when someone finds time. Our working: 60 invoices × 6 minutes saved = 360 minutes.

## What it runs on

Xero, connected through its official integration, plus your inbox. AI writes the drafts. Which invoices get chased, and when, is decided by simple rules you set.

## What it doesn't do

It doesn't threaten, charge interest, stop accounts or send anything without approval.
