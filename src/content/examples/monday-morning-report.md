# One page on Monday morning that tells you how the business is doing

**Worked example.** The business and figures below are illustrative, based on a typical firm of this size. We measure yours in the first conversation.

## The business

A 30-person trades firm with four crews. The owner wants to know which jobs make money, where the leads come from, and whether cash is fine this month. The answers are spread across the job software, Xero and a quotes spreadsheet.

## How it works today

1. Every Monday the owner spends a couple of hours exporting and pasting into a spreadsheet.
2. Some weeks it doesn't get done.
3. Problems such as a job running over hours or a big invoice unpaid show up late.

## What we'd build

1. **Early on Monday, the figures are pulled automatically** from the job software, Xero and the quotes spreadsheet.
2. **The same calculations are run every week**: work won and lost, quote win rate by lead source, hours against estimate by job and crew, money owed and money due out.
3. **A one-page report is emailed to the owner by 7am**, with a short plain-English summary at the top.
4. **The three things that need a decision are listed first**, for example: "Job 2214 is 30% over its estimated hours."

## Where a person checks

- The calculations are fixed and visible, so the numbers are the same each week and can be traced to the source.
- The written summary only describes the figures. It doesn't invent explanations.
- The owner decides what to do about any of it.

## Time back

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Building the report | about 2 hours 15 minutes | none |
| Reading it | included above | about 15 minutes |

**About 2 hours a week back**, and a report that actually arrives every week. Our working: 2 hours 15 minutes of exporting and pasting, against 15 minutes of reading.

## What it runs on

Your existing job software and accounts, through their standard exports or integrations. The numbers come from ordinary calculations. AI writes only the short summary on top.

## What it doesn't do

It doesn't replace your accountant or make decisions. If a source system doesn't hold a figure, the report says so rather than estimating it.
