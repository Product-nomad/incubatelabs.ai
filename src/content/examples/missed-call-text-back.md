# Missed calls answered in under a minute, while you're under a sink

**Worked example.** The business and figures below are illustrative, based on a typical firm of this size. We measure yours in the first conversation.

## The business

A plumbing and heating firm with five engineers and one person in the office. When everyone is on a job, or it's after six, calls go unanswered. Leads also arrive from online directories by email, and the first firm to reply usually wins them.

## How it works today

1. Around 10 calls a week are missed.
2. Some leave a voicemail. Many don't, and ring the next firm on the list.
3. Directory leads sit in an inbox until someone has a moment.

## What we'd build

1. **When a call is missed, the customer gets a text within a minute.** It says clearly that it's an automated assistant, then asks what they need and where they are.
2. **Their replies are gathered into one enquiry**: name, address, problem, how urgent, and the best time to call back.
3. **The enquiry lands in the office inbox** and on the job list, with emergencies flagged to whoever is on call.
4. **Directory leads get the same treatment**: an instant, labelled reply and a tidy enquiry for the office.
5. **A person calls back** to book the job.

## Where a person checks

- The assistant never quotes prices or books jobs. A person does both.
- Every message says it's automated. Since August 2026 that's required anyway if any of your customers are in the EU. See [what your chatbot must say](/guides/eu-ai-act-article-50-chatbots/).
- Emergencies go straight to a person.

## What it's worth

This one is measured in enquiries rather than hours. Our working: if 10 calls a week go unanswered and about a third of those callers would otherwise ring a competitor, a fast reply keeps **around 3 enquiries a week** that would have been lost. Your phone system's missed-call log gives us the real figure.

## What it runs on

Your existing phone system, as long as it can send a missed-call alert (many business phone systems can; we check yours first), plus a text messaging service and your inbox. AI is used only to understand the customer's replies.

## What it doesn't do

It doesn't pretend to be a person, give advice about gas or electrical safety, or promise appointment times.

## How we'd start

A **Quick Win**: missed-call texts first, then directory leads once you're happy with how it talks to customers.
